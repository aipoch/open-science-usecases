# Can AI Extract Final Action Items, Owners, and Deadlines

## Archived study

Extracts final actions, owners, deadlines, and cancellation status from meeting excerpts containing corrections. The archived review checks the output against supplied excerpt identifiers and records reasons for each judgment.

## Interpretation and limits

This is a supplied-document extraction exercise, not evidence that any real action was assigned or completed. This package also appears upstream under the meeting-corrections case title; the two entries are not independent replications.

## Package and provenance

The original `.science` export and matching poster are preserved byte-for-byte from [the upstream case](https://github.com/aipoch/open-science-usecases/tree/a1d1bfcb7d1c7a85d3e4a465060fa87fddc6d284/Can%20AI%20Extract%20Final%20Action%20Items%2C%20Owners%2C%20and%20Deadlines). The package contains the conversation, session metadata, and the resources inventoried in its internal manifest. This introduction summarizes the archived record; the research, external sources, and model evaluations were not rerun for this import.
