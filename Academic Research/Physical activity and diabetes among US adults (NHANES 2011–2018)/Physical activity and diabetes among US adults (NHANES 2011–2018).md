# Physical activity and diabetes among US adults (NHANES 2011–2018)

## Archived study

Analyzes recreational activity and diabetes prevalence in **18,241 NHANES adults from 2011–2018**. The high-activity versus no-activity prevalence gap narrowed from **−10.38 percentage points** with survey weighting to **−2.73 points** after adjustment including BMI (95% CI −4.04 to −1.41).

## Interpretation and limits

These historical cross-sectional associations are not preventive treatment effects or current US prevalence estimates. Complete-case selection, reverse causation and self-reported activity limit interpretation.
