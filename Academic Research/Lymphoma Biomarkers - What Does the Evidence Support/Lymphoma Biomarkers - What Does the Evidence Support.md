## What the comparison shows

A plausible mechanism and a prognostic association are useful context, but neither demonstrates a marker-by-treatment interaction. The absence of a treatment-response endpoint in the public cohort is decisive for that distinction.

## Assumptions and limits

The cohort has no bispecific-treatment response endpoint, and the literature review is not exhaustive. Bulk RNA is not surface protein or a clinical threshold. Prognostic and treated-patient associations do not establish differential treatment benefit or a therapy-selection rule; clinical estimates were checked for transcription, not refitted.
