# How much does battery health decline after two years of smartphone use

## Research question

What can selected owner reports say about one model’s battery health after roughly two years?

## Data and method

Retain only reports identifying the phone model, an apparent original battery, and the defined use-duration range. Summarize the distribution rather than presenting one impressive or disappointing report as typical.

## Recorded findings

Summarizes self-reported battery-health observations for a selected phone model after roughly two years. The archive records selection criteria, descriptive statistics, and source discussions.

The 21-report archive has mean health 86.8%, median 86%, and an observed 81–94% range. Mean loss is 13.2 percentage points of the reported initial capacity.

## What the findings mean

The convenience sample is self-reported and may contain selection bias or uncertain ages. Percentage-point loss is not the same as a measured decline in everyday runtime.

## Interpretation and limits

These owner reports form a convenience sample, not a representative degradation study. Device history, reporting selection, and the battery-health estimate limit generalization to other phones.
