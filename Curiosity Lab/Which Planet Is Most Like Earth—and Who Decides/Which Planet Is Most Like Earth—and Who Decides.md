# Which Planet Is Most Like Earth—and Who Decides?

## Focus of the case

How do ties and weighting choices change an Earth-similarity ranking?

## Research topic and question

- Which Planet Is Most Like Earth——and Who Decides
- Download the latest NASA Exoplanet Archive data and compare rankings based on size, temperature and distance from Earth. Does changing what we value change the winner? Use https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/TAP/usingTAP.html and the archive’s parameter definitions.

## How to read the analysis

The preserved analysis separates radius, temperature, distance, and composite rankings. It retains the unmodified source response and makes the handling of missing data, scaling, ties, and weights visible.

## Research findings

The ranking depends on which properties are treated as most important:

- Size-only: eight planets tie at exactly 1.0 Earth radius.
- Temperature-only: HD 40307 g is the unique 255 K winner.
- Distance-only: Proxima Centauri b and d tie because they belong to the same system.
- All five composite rankings have unique winners.

## What this comparison helps explain

A named winner is conditional on the selected definition. A similarity ranking is not a demonstration of habitability.
