# CAN AI PICK LUCKY LOTTERY NUMBERS?

## Research question

Can historical frequency give a fixed lottery ticket an advantage over random or sequential numbers?

## Data and method

Choose the frequency ticket from pre-2016 draws, freeze two comparison tickets, and test all three on the same 626 draws from 2016–2021. Compare observed matches with exact hypergeometric probabilities and fair simulated histories, keeping post-hoc comparisons identifiable.

## Recorded findings

Locks a frequency-based lottery ticket using older draws, then compares it with fixed random and sequential tickets on later draws. Fair simulated draws and theoretical match odds provide a separate chance benchmark.

Total matches were 452 for the frequency ticket, 472 for the fixed random ticket, and 511 for the sequential ticket. Under the fair-draw model every fixed six-number combination has the same match distribution.

## What the findings mean

The sequential ticket’s historical lead is a realized fluctuation, not a change in its future odds. Repeated comparisons selected after seeing results weaken the interpretation of a surprising margin.

## Interpretation and limits

Historical ticket rankings and late-period streaks do not establish predictive skill. The recorded exercise excludes Superzahl and does not demonstrate a method for improving fair lottery odds.
