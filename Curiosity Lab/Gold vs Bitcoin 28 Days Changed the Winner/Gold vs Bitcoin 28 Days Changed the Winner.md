# Gold vs Bitcoin 28 Days Changed the Winner

## Research question

How sensitive is an equal-dollar asset comparison to the historical entry checkpoint?

## Data and method

Convert the same dollar amount into holdings at each supplied entry price and value them at matched checkpoints. Keep GLD and IBIT identified as fund proxies, and compare each entry separately rather than blending different holding intervals.

## Recorded findings

Values equal-dollar GLD and IBIT purchases using a supplied price table and compares several entry dates. The archived tables and charts illustrate how the winner changes with the chosen historical start.

The archive illustrates that the winning proxy changes with entry date; a favorable starting price can dominate an apparent asset preference.

## What the findings mean

The supplied checkpoints are not independent investment experiments. ETF proxies, selected dates, and the common exit define the result.

## Interpretation and limits

The analysis concerns the supplied checkpoints and ETF proxies. Entry-date sensitivity is not a prediction of future returns or a recommendation to buy either asset.
