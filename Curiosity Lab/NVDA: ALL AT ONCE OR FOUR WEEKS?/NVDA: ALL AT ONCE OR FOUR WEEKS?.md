# NVDA: ALL AT ONCE OR FOUR WEEKS?

## Research question

How often does earlier market exposure beat a short staged entry in the same historical stock?

## Data and method

Compare a lump sum with four weekly installments for monthly starting dates with complete one-year exits. Keep uninvested cash in portfolio wealth so the staged strategy is not credited only for its invested fraction.

## Recorded findings

Compares investing a lump sum in NVIDIA with four weekly installments over one-year holding periods. The record expands the monthly start-date comparison and includes uninvested cash in portfolio accounting.

The archive reports 80 lump-sum wins and 40 installment wins across 120 starts from 2015–2024. Winning frequencies differ across start years.

## What the findings mean

Overlapping windows and a single strongly trending stock are not independent draws from all future market conditions.

## Interpretation and limits

Results depend on supplied historical prices, start dates, cash treatment, and execution assumptions. The overlapping windows do not establish a universally preferable investment strategy.
