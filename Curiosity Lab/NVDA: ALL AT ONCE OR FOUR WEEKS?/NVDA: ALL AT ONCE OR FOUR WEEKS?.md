# NVDA: ALL AT ONCE OR FOUR WEEKS?

## Archived study

Compares investing a lump sum in NVIDIA with four weekly installments over one-year holding periods. The record expands the monthly start-date comparison and includes uninvested cash in portfolio accounting.

## Interpretation and limits

Results depend on supplied historical prices, start dates, cash treatment, and execution assumptions. The overlapping windows do not establish a universally preferable investment strategy.
