# NVDA: ALL AT ONCE OR FOUR WEEKS?

## Research question

How often does earlier market exposure beat a short staged entry in the same historical stock?

Compares investing a lump sum in NVIDIA with four weekly installments over one-year holding periods. The record expands the monthly start-date comparison and includes uninvested cash in portfolio accounting.

## Data and comparison design

The comparison uses a lump sum with four weekly installments for monthly starting dates with complete one-year exits. Keep uninvested cash in portfolio wealth so the staged strategy is not credited only for its invested fraction.

## Recorded findings

Using prices through 2025-12-31, all starts from Jan 2015–Dec 2024 have a full one-year window. Lump sum won 80/120 tests (66.7%); weekly DCA won 40/120 (33.3%).

Assumptions remain the same: first trading day of each month; four $2,500 buys at 0, 7, 14, and 21 days (moved to the next trading day where needed); adjusted-close valuation on or before the one-year anniversary; fractional shares; 0% return on temporarily uninvested cash; no taxes or trading costs. All DCA cash is invested by week four, well before the one-year endpoint.

## What the comparison shows

Overlapping windows and a single strongly trending stock are not independent draws from all future market conditions.

## Assumptions and limits

Results depend on supplied historical prices, start dates, cash treatment, and execution assumptions. The overlapping windows do not establish a universally preferable investment strategy.
