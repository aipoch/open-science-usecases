# Can 20 Pokémon Packs Guarantee an SIR

## Archived study

Examines reported Special Illustration Rare pulls and illustrates the chance of opening a run of packs without a hit. The conversation distinguishes observed sample rates from an assumed per-pack probability.

## Interpretation and limits

The sample is not an official guarantee and may not represent all packs. The dry-streak calculation assumes independent packs with a constant probability; it does not ensure a hit after any fixed number of purchases.
