# For the same one-square-meter area, which receives more solar energy over the course of a year London or Seattle

## Archived study

Compares matched-period sunshine-duration normals for London and Seattle and exports the standardized annual totals. The session explains its conversion to a one-square-metre illustrative comparison.

## Interpretation and limits

Sunshine duration is a proxy, not measured global horizontal irradiation. The one-sun-equivalent calculation must not be interpreted as actual annual solar energy received by a panel.
