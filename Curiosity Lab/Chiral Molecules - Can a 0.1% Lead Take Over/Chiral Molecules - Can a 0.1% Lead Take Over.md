# Chiral Molecules - Can a 0.1% Lead Take Over

## Research question

When can a tiny left-right imbalance amplify in an explicitly defined reaction model?

## Data and method

Write reversible autocatalysis and mutual inhibition as separate reactions, check stoichiometry, and compare the amplification condition with a control. Keep precursor supply, sink, and washout assumptions visible rather than treating them as facts about every chemical system.

## Recorded findings

Explores amplification of a small molecular handedness imbalance in a simplified autocatalysis and inhibition model. The conversation revisits the cited reaction scheme and documents reactor and noise assumptions.

The source audit supports the reaction scheme and the g<1 amplification criterion under the modeled assumptions. The g=1 control cancels deterministic amplification without deleting the reaction network.

## What the findings mean

Independent environmental perturbations are not the same as correlated molecular reaction noise. A model amplification result is not evidence that a particular laboratory synthesis achieves it.

## Interpretation and limits

The source paper and modeled regime must be distinguished. Independent environmental perturbations are not chemical reaction noise, and a model demonstration is not evidence of a measured chemical outcome.
