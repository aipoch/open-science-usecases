# CAN ONE WEEK FLIP THE WINNER?

## Research question

Can buying the same assets five shared trading days apart change the historical winner?

## Data and method

Pair each eligible entry with the entry five shared trading days earlier, then give each its own one-year exit. Use the same price series and initial capital. A fixed common exit would instead compare unequal holding periods and answer a different question.

## Recorded findings

Compares NVIDIA and Bitcoin purchases made five shared trading days apart, with a separate one-year exit for each entry. A corrected export records paired dates, ending values, and changes in the historical winner.

The corrected archive reports 43 winner flips among 1,258 eligible starts, or 3.42%: 24 from Bitcoin to NVIDIA and 19 in the other direction.

## What the findings mean

Nearby starts and overlapping one-year windows are dependent comparisons. The flip rate describes this historical window rather than the probability of choosing a future winner.

## Interpretation and limits

The final correction replaces the earlier exit-date comparison. Overlapping holding periods, supplied prices, and a selected historical window limit interpretation; the result is not investment advice.
