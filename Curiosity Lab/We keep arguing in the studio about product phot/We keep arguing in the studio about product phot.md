# We keep arguing in the studio about product phot

## Archived study

Prepares a study of whether product-photo backgrounds change perceived price while holding the product image fixed. The archive contains an evidence-status report, extraction template, proposed experiment, analysis script, and illustrative figure.

## Interpretation and limits

The literature connector lacked credentials, so no published background-only percentage premium was verified. Candidate sources remain unverified and the proposed participant study was not run.

## Package and provenance

The original `.science` export and matching poster are preserved byte-for-byte from [the upstream case](https://github.com/aipoch/open-science-usecases/tree/a1d1bfcb7d1c7a85d3e4a465060fa87fddc6d284/We%20keep%20arguing%20in%20the%20studio%20about%20product%20phot). The package contains the conversation, session metadata, and the resources inventoried in its internal manifest. This introduction summarizes the archived record; the research, external sources, and model evaluations were not rerun for this import.
