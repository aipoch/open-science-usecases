# We keep arguing in the studio about product phot

## Research question

Can a background-only experiment isolate changes in perceived product price?

Prepares a study of whether product-photo backgrounds change perceived price while holding the product image fixed. The archive contains an evidence-status report, extraction template, proposed experiment, analysis script, and illustrative figure.

## Data and comparison design

The proposed 2×2 design varies background material and context while holding the product image fixed. It defines willingness to pay and a log-scale contrast whose percentage interpretation is 100 × (exp(beta) − 1).

## Recorded findings

| Comparison element | Controlled protocol | Recorded evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment | Change the product-photo background | Keep the product and other presentation elements fixed. |
| Outcome | Estimate the background-only price premium | No verified numerical premium was established. |
| Delivered methods | Protocol, analysis code and evidence template | A study design ready for examination. |
| Figure | Illustrative figure | Not a plotted measurement of an observed treatment effect. |

The archive delivers a protocol, analysis code, evidence template, and illustrative figure. No verified numerical background-only premium was established because the literature connection lacked access.

The research question is a background-only effect: would a consumer value the same product differently if its photograph used a different background? Product quality, lighting, camera angle, audience and sales channel would otherwise confound the comparison. The archive provides a protocol, analysis code and an evidence template. It does not establish a measured percentage premium; the illustrative figure is a proposed display format rather than a result from a completed price experiment.

## What the comparison shows

A design and a schematic are not an executed participant experiment. The draft should not attach an effect size to the illustrative image.

## Assumptions and limits

The literature connector lacked credentials, so no published background-only percentage premium was verified. Candidate sources remain unverified and the proposed participant study was not run.
