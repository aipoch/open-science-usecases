# Bottled Water in Convenience Stores Across Five Cities- What Is the Price Gap Between the Most Expensive and the Cheapest

## Archived study

Compares sampled convenience-store bottled-water prices across five Australian cities after standardizing the listed bottle volume. The archived output includes product, store, date, source, and price information.

## Interpretation and limits

Online delivery-menu prices, brands, and stores differ. This is a small store-level snapshot, not a representative citywide price index or a current shopping recommendation.
