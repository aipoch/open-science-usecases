# Bottled Water in Convenience Stores Across Five Cities- What Is the Price Gap Between the Most Expensive and the Cheapest

## Research question

How different are prices when bottle size and product type are held comparable?

Compares sampled convenience-store bottled-water prices across five Australian cities after standardizing the listed bottle volume. The archived output includes product, store, date, source, and price information.

## Data and comparison design

Standardize the sampled products to 600 mL of plain still water and retain the store, product, collection date, and menu source for each observation. Compute both the high/low price ratio and the difference relative to the lower price; these describe different presentations of the same contrast.

## Recorded findings

Using five Australian cities and one representative convenience store in each, the cheapest single bottle of plain still water (500–750 mL) was:

| City | Convenience store sampled | Cheapest standard bottle | Price (AUD) |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Sydney | Eats 2 U, Berkshire Park | Cool Ridge, 600 mL | $4.00 |
| Melbourne | Eats 2 U, Maribyrnong | Cool Ridge, 600 mL | $3.60 |
| Brisbane | Eats 2 U, Tingalpa | Cool Ridge, 600 mL | $3.85 |
| Adelaide | QuickStop by UP, Findon | United Water, 600 mL | $3.50 |
| Perth | Scarboro Deli | Mt Franklin, 600 mL | $4.50 |

Sources: [Sydney](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/eats-2-u-by-eg-berkshire-park/ks-v944sVuiUVa9yd4f2AA), [Melbourne](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/eats-2-u-by-eg-maribyrnong/NFcxzg4bR_CgkF9UXhYLoA), [Brisbane](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/eats-2-u-by-eg-tingalpa/8gAiO-_nSmiusRvypCKuoA), [Adelaide](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/quickstop-by-up-findon/X3EGvnx9U5mWbwd9LU6SpA/8a2b53bc-1a78-4310-a8f4-315108b9fd36), and [Perth](https://www.ubereats.com/au/store/scarboro-deli/eRpgGmfzUfKnsC7mVAT85A/1ed3a588-6a6e-4622-874e-c8a064c5efec).

**Result:** Perth was the most expensive at $4.50, while Adelaide was the cheapest at $3.50.



`Price multiple = 4.50 / 3.50 = 1.29`



Thus, the most expensive bottle cost approximately **1.29 times** as much as the cheapest—about **29% more**.

This is a store-level snapshot based on listed delivery-menu prices, which may include platform markups; it should not be interpreted as a definitive citywide price comparison.

## What the comparison shows

Five online delivery-menu observations are a bounded store comparison, not a representative estimate of citywide retail prices.

## Assumptions and limits

Online delivery-menu prices, brands, and stores differ. This is a small store-level snapshot, not a representative citywide price index or a current shopping recommendation.
