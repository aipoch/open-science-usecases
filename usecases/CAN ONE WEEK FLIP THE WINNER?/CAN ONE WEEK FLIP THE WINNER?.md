# CAN ONE WEEK FLIP THE WINNER?

## Archived study

Compares NVIDIA and Bitcoin purchases made five shared trading days apart, with a separate one-year exit for each entry. A corrected export records paired dates, ending values, and changes in the historical winner.

## Interpretation and limits

The final correction replaces the earlier exit-date comparison. Overlapping holding periods, supplied prices, and a selected historical window limit interpretation; the result is not investment advice.
