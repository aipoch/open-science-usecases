# First Flight vs. Evening Flight - Which Is More Reliable

## Archived study

Compares first morning flights with evening departures from JFK, LaGuardia, and Newark using a supplied July 2026 BTS reporting-carrier file. The archived analysis matches routes and airlines, includes cancellations in its disruption measure, and checks an alternative definition of morning as before 09:00.

The conversation ends by rerunning the saved script against the saved source ZIP and synchronizing the derived tables, figures, report, and README. That correction documents an independently reset bootstrap seed after an earlier mismatch in the sensitivity-analysis confidence intervals.

## Interpretation and limits

This is an observational comparison for one month and three airports, not a randomized estimate or a guarantee about a future flight. Matching routes and airlines does not remove every difference between departure times. The archived source, definitions, exclusions, and uncertainty calculations should accompany any use of its findings.
