# Chiral Molecules - Can a 0.1% Lead Take Over

## Archived study

Explores amplification of a small molecular handedness imbalance in a simplified autocatalysis and inhibition model. The conversation revisits the cited reaction scheme and documents reactor and noise assumptions.

## Interpretation and limits

The source paper and modeled regime must be distinguished. Independent environmental perturbations are not chemical reaction noise, and a model demonstration is not evidence of a measured chemical outcome.
