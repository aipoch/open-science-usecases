# CAN AI PICK LUCKY LOTTERY NUMBERS?

## Archived study

Locks a frequency-based lottery ticket using older draws, then compares it with fixed random and sequential tickets on later draws. Fair simulated draws and theoretical match odds provide a separate chance benchmark.

## Interpretation and limits

Historical ticket rankings and late-period streaks do not establish predictive skill. The recorded exercise excludes Superzahl and does not demonstrate a method for improving fair lottery odds.
