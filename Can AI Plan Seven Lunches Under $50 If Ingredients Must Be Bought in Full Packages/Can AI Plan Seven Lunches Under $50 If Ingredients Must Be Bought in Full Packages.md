# Can AI Plan Seven Lunches Under $50 If Ingredients Must Be Bought in Full Packages

## Archived study

Plans seven lunches with a fixed budget while requiring whole-package ingredient purchases and limiting recipe repeats. The archived answer includes a shopping list, serving arithmetic, and a proposed lowest-cost combination.

## Interpretation and limits

Prices and serving capacities are supplied synthetic inputs. This demonstrates constraint accounting, not current grocery prices, nutritional adequacy, or a generally optimal meal plan.
