# Can AI Read the Numbers from One Chart

## Research question

Can the plotted observations be recovered as a usable table with their units intact?

Reads eight labeled observations from a supplied line chart and returns their values with units. The package preserves the input and the tabular response for inspection.

## Data and comparison design

The report distinguishes each labeled point, preserve observation order, and carry the response-time unit into the output table. Keep chart transcription separate from explaining a trend or estimating values not directly shown.

The output is a transcription table, so observation labels and units matter as much as the values. W6 is the largest recorded value at 45 minutes and W3 the smallest at 11 minutes; those comparisons follow directly from the table rather than an interpretation of visual emphasis. The archive supplies one chart-reading response without an independently authenticated source-data table or a multi-chart benchmark. It therefore supports inspection of this transcription, not a general chart-reading accuracy score.

## What the comparison shows

These eight readings describe one supplied chart. The task does not establish accuracy on unlabeled, low-resolution, or differently scaled figures.

## Assumptions and limits

This is a single chart-reading example. The archived answer does not establish accuracy across other chart designs or independent measurement of ambiguous pixels.
