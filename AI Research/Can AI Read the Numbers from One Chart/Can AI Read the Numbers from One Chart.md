# Can AI Read the Numbers from One Chart

## Research question

Can the plotted observations be recovered as a usable table with their units intact?

## Data and method

Read each labeled point, preserve observation order, and carry the response-time unit into the output table. Keep chart transcription separate from explaining a trend or estimating values not directly shown.

## Recorded findings

Reads eight labeled observations from a supplied line chart and returns their values with units. The package preserves the input and the tabular response for inspection.

The archived table reports W1–W8 as 16, 26, 11, 36, 22, 45, 31, and 19 minutes.

## What the findings mean

These eight readings describe one supplied chart. The task does not establish accuracy on unlabeled, low-resolution, or differently scaled figures.

## Interpretation and limits

This is a single chart-reading example. The archived answer does not establish accuracy across other chart designs or independent measurement of ambiguous pixels.
