# Can AI Fit 10 London Landmarks Into One Day

## Archived study

Checks whether a proposed ten-landmark London itinerary fits an eight-hour day after adding visit durations, opening hours, and travel time. The archived calculation contrasts the initial proposal with a reduced feasible schedule.

## Interpretation and limits

The timings and opening-hour evidence belong to the archived scenario. One itinerary failure is not a general model capability estimate or a current travel recommendation.
