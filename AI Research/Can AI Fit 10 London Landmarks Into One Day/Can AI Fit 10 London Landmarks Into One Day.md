# Can AI Fit 10 London Landmarks Into One Day

## Research question

Will a plausible sightseeing list remain feasible after durations, travel, and opening windows are added?

## Data and method

Timestamp each arrival and departure rather than checking only the total visit time. Evaluate the original ten-stop proposal and a smaller route with the same day budget, coordinates, opening constraints, and chosen travel estimates.

## Recorded findings

Checks whether a proposed ten-landmark London itinerary fits an eight-hour day after adding visit durations, opening hours, and travel time. The archived calculation contrasts the initial proposal with a reduced feasible schedule.

The archived feasibility check rejects the ten-stop proposal and reports that six venues would be closed on arrival. A five-stop alternative fits the modeled eight-hour budget with a buffer.

## What the findings mean

This is one itinerary under its stated travel assumptions. It illustrates constraint accounting, not a general limit of five London visits per day.

## Interpretation and limits

The timings and opening-hour evidence belong to the archived scenario. One itinerary failure is not a general model capability estimate or a current travel recommendation.
