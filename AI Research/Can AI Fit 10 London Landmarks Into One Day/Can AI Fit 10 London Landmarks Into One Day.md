# Can AI Fit 10 London Landmarks Into One Day

## Research question

Will a plausible sightseeing list remain feasible after durations, travel, and opening windows are added?

Checks whether a proposed ten-landmark London itinerary fits an eight-hour day after adding visit durations, opening hours, and travel time. The archived calculation contrasts the initial proposal with a reduced feasible schedule.

## Data and comparison design

The analysis assigns arrival and departure times to the proposed stops, including visit durations, travel estimates and opening windows. The ten-stop proposal and reduced alternative use the same eight-hour day budget. This tests whether an apparently reasonable list is a feasible schedule; it does not assume that adding visit durations alone is enough.

## Recorded findings

The supplied ten-stop proposal fails two different constraints. Its archived timing check exceeds the eight-hour day, and six venues would already be closed on arrival. A reduced five-stop alternative is recorded as 6.9 hours including travel, with a 1.1-hour buffer and all arrivals inside the specified opening windows.

| Schedule | Archived duration | Opening-hour check | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original ten stops | 14.6 hours reported | Six venues closed on arrival | Infeasible |
| Reduced five stops | 6.9 hours reported | All arrivals fit the specified windows | Feasible under the modeled assumptions |

The source also displays rounded visit/travel components for the original plan that do not add exactly to its stated total. The saved timeline should therefore be checked before treating the displayed total as precision beyond this feasibility comparison. That rounding issue does not turn an approximately fifteen-hour plan into an eight-hour one. The case illustrates why a list of attractions and a timed itinerary are different outputs.

## What the comparison shows

This is one itinerary under its stated travel assumptions. It illustrates constraint accounting, not a general limit of five London visits per day.

## Assumptions and limits

The timings and opening-hour evidence belong to the archived scenario. One itinerary failure is not a general model capability estimate or a current travel recommendation.
