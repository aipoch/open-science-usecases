# Does AI Practice Translate into Independent Learning

**Creator:** @cheery9998

## Research question

Do improvements while a tool is available survive an independent assessment after it is removed?

## Data and method

Separate the assisted practice phase from the later closed-book phase, identify treatment encodings, and distinguish a student from a student-session record. Apply the stated Honors exclusion before calculating group summaries.

## Recorded findings

Examines whether improved performance during AI-assisted practice carries over to an independent assessment after AI is removed. The archived analysis distinguishes Control, GPT Base, and GPT Tutor, maps the supplied treatment encodings, excludes Honors classes from the main analysis, and summarizes practice and examination scores.

The archived workflow supplies group summaries and a comparison with the paper’s analysis. This draft keeps the comparison descriptive without reinstating previously removed unverified numerical claims.

## What the findings mean

Raw mean differences and covariate-adjusted causal estimates must not be presented as the same result. Task assistance can improve an immediate score without demonstrating transferable learning.

## Scope

The analysis separates assisted-practice performance from independent examination performance. Descriptive group means are distinct from adjusted causal estimates.
