# AI Explains Math - What Gets Lost

## Research question

When a theorem becomes a plain-language explanation, which assumptions or quantifiers stop being explicit?

## Data and method

Build item-level checklists from the source statements before classifying the explanations. Distinguish an omitted explicit condition from a changed mathematical scope, and distinguish definitions and conclusions from assumptions. The archive also checks when response collection and rule freezing happened.

## Recorded findings

Studies which conditions survive plain-English explanations of ten mathematical statements. The record progresses from source passages and frozen checklists to an exploratory comparison of collected explanations.

The item review reports 59 meaning-retaining entries, 15 not explicitly stated, and one uncertain entry. No reviewed passage clearly demonstrated a scope error under the supplied rules; these mixed checklist categories are not an overall correctness rate.

## What the findings mean

This exploratory sample describes particular explanations under particular judgments. A readable paraphrase may preserve meaning implicitly, while an explicit checklist can still expose information a reader needs.

## Interpretation and limits

Checklist judgments distinguish omitted wording from demonstrated mathematical scope errors. Collection overlap, rules written after pilot responses, interpretation uncertainty, and lack of expert review limit generalization.
