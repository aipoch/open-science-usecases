# AI Explains Math - What Gets Lost

## Archived study

Studies which conditions survive plain-English explanations of ten mathematical statements. The record progresses from source passages and frozen checklists to an exploratory comparison of collected explanations.

## Interpretation and limits

Checklist judgments distinguish omitted wording from demonstrated mathematical scope errors. Collection overlap, rules written after pilot responses, interpretation uncertainty, and lack of expert review limit generalization.
