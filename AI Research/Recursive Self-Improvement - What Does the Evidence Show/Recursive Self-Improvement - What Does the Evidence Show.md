# Recursive Self-Improvement - What Does the Evidence Show

**Creator:** @dongxi_nlp

## Research question

Which feedback loops are supported by evidence, and which remain proposed research programs?

## Data and method

Organize methods and claims in a literature register, link each claim to its evidence family, and separate automated optimization, tool-building, and self-modification. Keep the conceptual feedback diagram distinct from an executed recursive improvement loop.

## Recorded findings

Organizes a literature-based investigation of recursive self-improvement, covering methods, evidence, trends, and economic constraints. The archived research package includes a survey, a methods taxonomy, a claims-evidence matrix, bibliographic records, a chronology, a conceptual feedback diagram, reproducible build materials, and an agenda of three falsifiable experiments.

The archive provides a survey, taxonomy, evidence matrix, chronology, and three falsifiable experiment proposals. The proposals remain unexecuted.
