# Can AI Find Three Contradictions in a 20-Page Presentation

## Research question

Can a reader identify conflicting claims and locate both sides of each discrepancy?

## Data and method

Compare statements referring to the same quantity, population, or release plan across the supplied deck. Report the slide pair and the two conflicting values, distinguishing an unexplained revision from a proven erroneous value.

## Recorded findings

Reviews a supplied presentation for conflicting statements and identifies the slide pairs involved. The archived answer distinguishes discrepancies in active users, pilot conversion, and a planned release date.

The recorded answer identifies slides 4/11 for 1.20 versus 1.05 million active users, 7/15 for 8% versus 12% conversion, and 9/18 for June versus September 2026 release timing.

## What the findings mean

A contradiction identifies a reconciliation task. It does not establish which slide is correct without the underlying source.

## Interpretation and limits

The result concerns this supplied deck. Conflicting statements need source reconciliation; identifying them does not determine which number or date is correct.
