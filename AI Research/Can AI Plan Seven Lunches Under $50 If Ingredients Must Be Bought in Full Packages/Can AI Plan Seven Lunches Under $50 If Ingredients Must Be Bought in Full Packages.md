# Can AI Plan Seven Lunches Under $50 If Ingredients Must Be Bought in Full Packages

## Research question

Is a seven-lunch budget feasible when groceries must be bought as whole packages?

## Data and method

Count purchased packages rather than pricing fractional recipe portions. Sum serving use across the week, enforce recipe-repeat limits, and retain leftovers in the inventory accounting. The optimization concerns the supplied package prices and capacities.

## Recorded findings

Plans seven lunches with a fixed budget while requiring whole-package ingredient purchases and limiting recipe repeats. The archived answer includes a shopping list, serving arithmetic, and a proposed lowest-cost combination.

The proposed combination costs $22.10 in seven packages and leaves $27.90 of the nominal budget. It uses three servings each of two noodle recipes and one chickpea-spinach pasta lunch.

## What the findings mean

The arithmetic is specific to synthetic prices and recipe assumptions. Feasibility here is not a verified local shopping basket or a nutritional assessment.

## Interpretation and limits

Prices and serving capacities are supplied synthetic inputs. This demonstrates constraint accounting, not current grocery prices, nutritional adequacy, or a generally optimal meal plan.
