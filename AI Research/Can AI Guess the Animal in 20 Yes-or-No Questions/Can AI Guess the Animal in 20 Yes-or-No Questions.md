# Can AI Guess the Animal in 20 Yes-or-No Questions

## Archived study

Preserves an interactive animal-guessing conversation using yes-or-no questions. The recorded exchange ends with a macaw identified within the requested question limit.

## Interpretation and limits

This is one conversation, not a controlled benchmark across animals or participants. The result depends on the answers provided during the exchange.

## Package and provenance

The original `.science` export and matching poster are preserved byte-for-byte from [the upstream case](https://github.com/aipoch/open-science-usecases/tree/a1d1bfcb7d1c7a85d3e4a465060fa87fddc6d284/Can%20AI%20Guess%20the%20Animal%20in%2020%20Yes-or-No%20Questions). This introduction summarizes the archived record; the research, external sources, and model evaluations were not rerun for this import.
